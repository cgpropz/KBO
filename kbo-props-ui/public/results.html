What 54.2% means

The break-even reference

54.2% is the reference break-even. It is a reference line, not a target or a forecast.

Beating it over a season does not mean every slip makes money. Results vary slip to slip, and small samples swing.

How grading works

Definitions

HIT Final stat lands on the posted side of the line.

MISS Final stat lands on the other side.

Push: the result landed exactly on the line; it counts in W-L-P but not in the hit rate.

Void: the pick was cancelled or the player didn't play; it's left out of the record.
