How to read the board: CG Score and hit rates

CG Score shows how far the CGPropz projection sits from the posted line. Around 50 means the projection and the line are roughly even, above 50 leans over, and below 50 leans under. A bigger gap from 50 means a bigger gap between the model and the line. Scores on low lines can run higher. It's a measure of that gap, not a probability, and not a claim that higher scores hit more often. The board treats a prop as an over when the projection is at or above the line. How CG Score works →

Hit rates count how often a player has cleared today's line in a recent window. The CGPropz WNBA board shows several:

  • L10: the player's last ten games against today's line.
  • Season: every game this season against today's line.
  • H2H: games against today's opponent.

Read every hit rate alongside its sample size. "Cleared in most of the last ten" based on a few games is much weaker than the same share over a full season. H2H samples are usually the smallest of the three.

DVP (defense vs. position) ranks how the opponent has defended the player's position. It's context for the matchup, not a verdict on its own.

What changes in a Finals series

Regular-season numbers don't carry over cleanly into a seven-game series. Before you lean on any projection or hit rate, check these:

  • Minutes concentrate. Coaches shorten rotations in the Finals. Starters often play more and bench players less, which moves projections for both groups. A season hit rate built on regular-season minutes may understate or overstate a player's role now.
  • Rotations and matchups adjust game to game. After each game, both staffs change coverages and assignments. A player who was the focus of the defense in one game may see a different look in the next.
  • Injury and rest news is the biggest swing factor. One player ruled out or limited changes usage for everyone else on that team. Check the official injury report and the late lineup news before tip-off. Lines that were posted before the news may not reflect it.
  • Samples are small. By Game 3, a head-to-head sample in this series is two games. That is too few to read as a trend, and one hot or cold shooting night can swing it.
  • Rest after sweeps. Both teams had a week off before Game 1. Early-series form can differ from late-semifinal form.
  • Home and away. The series moves between Chase Center and Atlanta. Watch whether the board's numbers shift with the venue.

Using pick'em boards responsibly

  • Set a budget before the series starts and keep to it, whatever happens in any single game.
  • Re-check the line in your app before entering. Pick'em lines move as news breaks. The lines on CGPropz are the lines at the time the board was updated.
  • Don't chase. A miss in Game 1 doesn't make Game 2 more likely to go your way.
  • Treat every pick as uncertain. A high CG Score means a large gap between projection and line. It does not mean the prop will hit.
  • Know the rules where you live. Pick'em contest eligibility varies by state, and age requirements apply.
