KBO postseason 2026: schedule and first pitch in ET

Korea is 13 hours ahead of US Eastern Time until Nov 1, when US clocks fall back and the gap becomes 14 hours. A 6:30 PM game in Korea starts at 5:30 AM ET (4:30 AM ET from Nov 2). A 2:00 PM game in Korea starts at 1:00 AM ET on the same calendar date.

What changes for props in the postseason

  • One game a day. Fewer props on the board, and fewer free picks to choose from.
  • Pitching usage changes. Starters can be pulled early and top relievers used more often, which matters for strikeout and pitching-out lines.
  • Lineups get tighter. Regular-season samples may not reflect postseason roles. Check the posted lineup before the game.
  • Lines may not be posted for every game. Whether pick'em apps post KBO postseason props is up to them.

How the KBO board works

The live KBO board in the app lines a model number up with the posted prop. The free preview shows three lines. The rest of that live board is All-Access, and those rows are not printed here. Graded results for days already in the archive are listed below, including misses.

How the KBO free top 3 is picked: KBO's free picks are chosen on the server. They do not follow the site's CG Score formula, so they aren't simply the top three CG Scores on the board.

What CG Score means on KBO

CG Score compares the CGPropz projection with the posted line. KBO works a little differently from the other sports:

  • When a KBO prop has a stored KBO score, the board shows that score.
  • When it doesn't, the score is the projection (or the player's season average if there's no projection) divided by the line, times 50. On that basis, 50 means the projection equals the line.

Until that's confirmed, don't read 50 on a stored KBO score as "projection equals line."

The score is not a probability, and a higher score is not a claim that the prop hits more often. How CG Score works is the full guide.

FAQ

Are there KBO player props during the playoffs?

The KBO postseason runs from Oct 14 to as late as Nov 10, with one game a day. When lines are posted, the KBO board covers them, and the free top 3 appears on the homepage.

What time do KBO playoff games start in ET?

Most start at 5:30 AM ET. Weekend games in the first two rounds start at 1:00 AM ET. After US clocks change on Nov 1, weekday Korean Series games start at 4:30 AM ET.

Where are today's free KBO picks?

In the free top 3 on the homepage. KBO's free picks are chosen on the server and don't simply follow the CG Score order.

What does CG Score mean on KBO?

When a KBO prop has a stored KBO score, the board shows it. Otherwise it's the projection divided by the line, times 50. A higher score is not a probability or a claim that the prop hits more often.

What is a full-board result?

Every prop the model graded that day, including hits, misses, pushes and DNPs. It is the whole graded board for that date, not a short list of winners.

What time zone are the results dates?

Korea (KST) game dates, not US Eastern Time.

Where is pricing?

All-Access plans are on the pricing page.

Is CGPropz affiliated with PrizePicks or Underdog?
