KBO player props, with the full board.

CGPropz tracks KBO player props for Korea Baseball Organization games: batter lines such as hits, runs, and RBIs, and pitcher lines such as strikeouts. Each prop sits next to a model number built from recent form, the matchup, and the posted line. The live board is on cgpropz.com. All-Access plans that include KBO are listed on pricing.
