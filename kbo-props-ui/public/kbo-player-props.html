KBO postseason 2026: schedule and first pitch in ET

Korea is 13 hours ahead of US Eastern Time until Nov 1, when US clocks fall back and the gap becomes 14 hours. A 6:30 PM game in Korea starts at 5:30 AM ET (4:30 AM ET from Nov 2). A 2:00 PM game in Korea starts at 1:00 AM ET on the same calendar date.

What changes for props in the postseason

  • One game a day. Fewer props on the board, and fewer free picks to choose from.
  • Pitching usage changes. Starters can be pulled early and top relievers used more often, which matters for strikeout and pitching-out lines.
  • Lineups get tighter. Regular-season samples may not reflect postseason roles. Check the posted lineup before the game.
  • Lines may not be posted for every game. Whether pick'em apps post KBO postseason props is up to them.

What CG Score means on KBO

CG Score shows how far the CGPropz projection sits from the posted line. Around 50 means the projection and the line are roughly even, above 50 leans over, and below 50 leans under. A bigger gap from 50 means a bigger gap between the model and the line, and scores on low lines can run higher.

Sports can calculate it a bit differently. KBO can show its own stored score, so read KBO scores on their own terms rather than comparing them directly with other sports.

The score is not a probability, and a higher score is not a claim that the prop hits more often. How CG Score works is the full guide.
