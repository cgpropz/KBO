FAQ

What's included in the free tier?

You get the landing page, today's KBO & WNBA schedules, and a preview of the top 3 lines on the KBO, WNBA, NFL, and NHL boards. Upgrade to unlock full projections, prop cards, and the PrizePicks edge board for every sport.

Does one subscription cover every sport?

Yes — every All-Access plan (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime) unlocks KBO, WNBA, NFL, NBA, and NHL under one login. Discord Only is separate: props in Discord, not the site boards.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time, and your access continues until the end of your current billing period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with nothing to cancel. There is no free trial.

What is Discord Only?

A cheaper plan that posts the props in the CGPropz Discord and gives you the Discord props role. It does not unlock the site boards. All-Access includes the full site and the site-subscriber Discord role.
