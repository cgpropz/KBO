What CG Score means

CG Score compares the CGPropz projection to the line. The board also shows DVP and recent hit rates as matchup and form context. The further the score is from 50, the bigger the gap between projection and line. Above 50 leans over, below 50 leans under. It isn't a win probability. A+ through C grades are based on the score.
