What CG Score means

CG Score compares the CGPropz projection with the posted line. The method stated on the site is projection divided by the line, multiplied by 50. A score of 50 means the projection equals the line. It is not a probability, and a higher score is not a claim that the prop hits more often. Read how CG Score works for the full explanation, including what the score does not tell you.
