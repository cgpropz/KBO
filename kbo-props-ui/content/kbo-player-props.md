# KBO player props, with the full board.

CGPropz tracks KBO player props for Korea Baseball Organization games: batter lines such as hits, runs and RBIs, and pitcher lines such as strikeouts. Each prop sits next to a model number built from recent form, the matchup and the posted line. The live board is the [KBO board](/?sport=kbo). All-Access plans that include KBO are listed on [pricing](/pricing).

## KBO postseason 2026: schedule and first pitch in ET

> {{CONFIRM SCHEDULE}}: dates and KST start times are from the KBO's Oct 7 announcement and totalbase.kr; ET conversions are ours. Postponed games move to the next day. Re-check before publishing.

Korea is 13 hours ahead of US Eastern Time until Nov 1, when US clocks fall back and the gap becomes 14 hours. A 6:30 PM game in Korea starts at **5:30 AM ET** (4:30 AM ET from Nov 2). A 2:00 PM game in Korea starts at **1:00 AM ET** on the same calendar date.

|---|---|---|---|
| Wild Card (up to 2 games) | No. 4 LG Twins vs No. 5 Doosan Bears, Jamsil Stadium | Wed Oct 14; Thu Oct 15 *(if needed)* | 5:30 AM |
| Semi-playoff (best of 5) | No. 3 Kia Tigers vs Wild Card winner | From Sat Oct 17 {{CONFIRM: one source says Oct 18}}: Oct 17, 18, 20, 21*, 23* | Sat/Sun 1:00 AM; weekdays 5:30 AM |
| Playoff (best of 5) | No. 2 Samsung Lions vs semi-playoff winner | Oct 25, 26, 28, 29*, 31* | Oct 25 and 31: 1:00 AM; others 5:30 AM |
| Korean Series (best of 7) | No. 1 KT Wiz vs playoff winner | Nov 2, 3, 5, 6, 7*, 9*, 10* | Weekdays 4:30 AM; Sat Nov 7: 12:00 AM (midnight Fri night ET); Game 7 {{CONFIRM}} |

\* If needed. 

The Wild Card works differently from the other rounds. LG, the fourth seed, advances with one win or one tie. Doosan has to win both games. In the Korean Series, KT hosts Games 1, 2, 6 and 7.

**What changes for props in the postseason**
- **One game a day.** Fewer props on the board, and fewer free picks to choose from.
- **Pitching usage changes.** Starters can be pulled early and top relievers used more often, which matters for strikeout and pitching-out lines.
- **Lineups get tighter.** Regular-season samples may not reflect postseason roles. Check the posted lineup before the game.
- **Lines may not be posted for every game.** Whether pick'em apps post KBO postseason props is up to them. {{CONFIRM: KBO postseason props availability on the board}}

## How the KBO board works

The live KBO board in the app lines a model number up with the posted prop. When KBO lines are posted, the free preview shows three of them. The rest of that live board is All-Access, and those rows are not printed here.

**How the KBO free top 3 is picked:** when KBO lines are posted, it is the three standard lines with the highest CG Scores on the board. Lines PrizePicks has taken down are left out.

## What CG Score means on KBO

CG Score shows how far the CGPropz projection sits from the posted line. Around 50 means the projection and the line are roughly even, above 50 leans over, and below 50 leans under. A bigger gap from 50 means a bigger gap between the model and the line, and scores on low lines can run higher.

Sports can calculate it a bit differently. KBO can show its own stored score, so read KBO scores on their own terms rather than comparing them directly with other sports.

The score is not a probability, and a higher score is not a claim that the prop hits more often. [How CG Score works](/guides/cg-score) is the full guide.

## FAQ

**Are there KBO player props during the playoffs?**
The KBO postseason runs from Oct 14 to as late as Nov 10, with one game a day. When lines are posted, the KBO board covers them, and the free top 3 appears on the homepage.

**What time do KBO playoff games start in ET?**
Most start at 5:30 AM ET. Weekend games in the first two rounds start at 1:00 AM ET. After US clocks change on Nov 1, weekday Korean Series games start at 4:30 AM ET.

**Are there free KBO picks?**
Only when KBO lines are posted. On those days the free top 3 on the homepage shows the three standard KBO lines with the highest CG Scores. With no KBO lines posted, there are no free KBO picks.
